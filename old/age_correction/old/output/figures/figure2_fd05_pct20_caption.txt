Figure S1. Model-implied age effects on neural distinctiveness, reliability
and confusability under FD 0.5 mm / 20% (liberal). Each panel plots change
from a reference rather than a level: every series is shifted by its own
fitted value at age 65 in the between-person column, and at the first wave in
the within-person column, so all series pass through zero there. Colour
encodes modality; the pooled series is the heavier near-black line. Ribbon:
95% CI of the model-implied change from the reference, zero width at the
reference by construction. Dashed segments span the unsampled age range
between the young and older samples. Within-person modality lines are shown
descriptively and at reduced weight, because the within-person age x modality
interaction was not significant for any outcome (distinctiveness F(2, 631.4)
= 1.27, p = 0.281; reliability F(2, 623.8) = 1.63, p = 0.196; confusability
F(2, 661.8) = 0.86, p = 0.423).
