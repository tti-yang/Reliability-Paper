Figure S2. Pooled magnitude contrast of the age effects on reliability and
confusability under FD 0.5 mm / 20% (liberal). Points are delta = |b_R| -
|b_C|, where b_R is the age slope for reliability and b_C the corresponding
slope for confusability, both per year on the original correlation scale.
delta is sign-free: a positive value means the age effect is larger in
magnitude for reliability, whichever direction either component points in.
Intervals are 95% bootstrap percentile intervals from a cluster bootstrap
over participants (B = 5,000 draws). Percentile intervals need not be
symmetric about the point estimate.
