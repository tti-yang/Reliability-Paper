Figure S1. Model-implied age effects on neural distinctiveness, reliability
and confusability under FD 0.5 mm / 20% (liberal). Each panel plots change
from a reference rather than a level: every series is shifted by its own
fitted value at age 65 in the between-person column, and at the first wave in
the within-person column, so all series pass through zero there. Colour
encodes modality; the pooled series is the heavier near-black line, from the
manuscript's pooled mixed model (same model as the fixed-effects table).
Ribbon: 95% CI of the model-implied change from the reference, zero width at
the reference by construction. Dashed segments span the unsampled age range
between the young and older samples. Colored modality-specific lines in both
columns are descriptive only, from visualization-only fits allowing different
slopes by modality; they carry no inferential interaction test, simple-slope
estimate, or modality-specific statistical conclusion.
