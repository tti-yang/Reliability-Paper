Figure 3. Pooled magnitude contrast of the age effects on reliability and
confusability under FD 0.4 mm / 10% (primary). Points are delta = |b_R| -
|b_C|, where b_R is the age slope for reliability and b_C the corresponding
slope for confusability, both per year on the original correlation scale.
delta is sign-free: a positive value means the age effect is larger in
magnitude for reliability, whichever direction either component points in.
Intervals are 95% bootstrap percentile intervals from a cluster bootstrap
over participants (B = 5,000 draws). The intervals are asymmetric about their
points because the absolute confusability slope is biased upward when the
true slope is near zero, which shifts the bootstrap distribution of the
contrast toward zero; the point estimates therefore sit above the centre of
their intervals, and the intervals are conservative.
